# Specialist Skill Boundary

A specialist skill may choose **how** to perform its specialty inside the authority granted by the active lifecycle phase.

It must not:
- reopen resolved user decisions;
- alter `SPEC_ACCEPTANCE`;
- alter Ticket scope, dependencies, semantic boundaries, or topology;
- alter a Frozen Review Contract;
- add or weaken blocking criteria;
- declare the overall Ticket `PASS`;
- expand scope implicitly.

A tool/backend skill may execute bounded work or obtain evidence, but it inherits the same restrictions.
